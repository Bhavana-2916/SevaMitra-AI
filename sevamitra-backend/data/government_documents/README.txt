SevaMitra final 18-sector corpus starter pack.

This pack is organized around the National Portal's major service categories and adds a dedicated Andhra Pradesh/state-aware sector plus a RAG policy document.

Important:
- It is a broad sector corpus starter, not a literal copy of every government service in India.
- Official portals remain authoritative for current/state-specific details.
- Re-ingest the PDFs after replacing your old corpus.
- Keep .env, venv, node_modules and secrets out of GitHub.
